# Purpose

This document establishes the foundational principles, business assumptions, product objectives, user groups, operational philosophy, and strategic decisions that guide the development of the Crazy Capital platform.

This document serves as the baseline reference for all future product, design, engineering, workflow, and operational decisions.

---

# Product Definition

Crazy Capital is a technology-enabled business operating system that provides access to financial, legal, compliance, taxation, insurance, investment, advisory, certification, government, and growth-related services through a unified platform.

The platform connects customers, partners, employees, and service providers through centralized workflows, CRM-driven operations, and standardized service delivery processes.

# Core Product Thesis

Businesses and individuals should not have to manage multiple service providers for different business and financial requirements.

Instead:

```text
One Customer
↓
One Platform
↓
Multiple Services
↓
Unified Experience
```

Crazy Capital becomes the single relationship layer between the customer and all required services.

# Product Principles

All platform decisions must align with the following principles.

---

## Principle 1

### Customer First

Customers should never feel they are interacting with multiple organizations.

Regardless of how services are delivered internally, the customer experience should remain unified.

---

## Principle 2

### Lead First

Lead capture is more important than immediate monetization.

Every interaction should create a lead record.

Examples:

- Contact Form
- Service Inquiry
- Callback Request
- Consultation Request
- Partner Referral

All should generate leads.

---

## Principle 3

### CRM Centric Operations

The CRM is the heart of the business.

Everything originates from:

```text
Lead
↓
Customer
↓
Service
↓
Workflow
↓
Delivery
```

Nothing should operate outside CRM.

---

## Principle 4

### Workflow Driven Execution

Every service must follow a workflow.

No service should be managed manually without workflow visibility.

Example:

```text
Lead
↓
Document Collection
↓
Verification
↓
Processing
↓
Completion
```

Workflows must remain configurable.

---

## Principle 5

### Service Standardization

Services should be standardized across:

- Branches
- Employees
- Partners

This ensures consistent delivery.

---

## Principle 6

### Partner Enabled Scale

Growth should not depend entirely on internal teams.

Partners should act as a distribution layer.

Examples:

- CA Partners
- Legal Partners
- Insurance Partners
- Loan Partners
- Referral Partners

---

## Principle 7

### Technology Driven Operations

Technology should reduce operational dependency.

The platform should automate:

- Assignments
- Notifications
- Tracking
- Reporting
- Escalations

wherever possible.

# Platform Operating Model

The platform operates using a centralized control structure.

```text
Lead
↓
Central Team
↓
Assignment
↓
Employee / Partner
↓
Execution
↓
Completion
```

This ensures:

- Lead ownership
- Quality control
- Standardization

# Product Constraints

The following constraints apply.

---

## Regulatory Constraints

Certain activities require licensed partners.

Examples:

- Insurance Distribution
- Mutual Fund Distribution
- Loan Origination

Phase 1 will operate through partner networks.

---

## Operational Constraints

Service delivery may involve:

- Internal Teams
- External Partners
- Third Party Vendors

The platform must support all three.

---

## Technical Constraints

The platform must:

- Remain cloud native
- Be modular
- Support future mobile applications
- Support future automation
