# Crazy Capital Product Discovery Package

Status: Approved for Product Discovery & Architecture Design  
Document Owner: Crazy Capital Product, Business & Technology Team  
Last Updated: August 2026

This folder is the living source of truth for the Crazy Capital product discovery process. All product decisions, business assumptions, workflows, domain definitions, access models, design assessments, and architecture discoveries must be maintained within these documents.

As the platform evolves, decisions should be updated in the relevant document rather than creating conflicting versions elsewhere. This ensures a single source of truth for business, product, design, engineering, and implementation teams.

The objective of this package is to establish complete product clarity before development, enabling efficient execution within Antigravity and ensuring alignment between business stakeholders, product teams, designers, and engineers.

The discovery package covers the entire Crazy Capital operating model, including:

- B2C Financial Services
- B2B Financial Services
- CRM & Lead Management
- Customer Management
- Partner Ecosystem
- Workflow Management
- Service Delivery Operations
- Reporting & Analytics
- Platform Administration
- Future Government & Fintech Integrations

# Product Vision

Crazy Capital is building a unified financial services platform that simplifies access to financial, compliance, taxation, lending, insurance, investment, and business services through a technology-driven operating system.

The platform serves:

- Individual Customers (B2C)
- Businesses & Startups (B2B)
- Financial Consultants
- Channel Partners
- Franchise Partners
- Internal Operations Teams
- Service Experts

The long-term vision is to become India's most trusted financial services ecosystem by combining technology, workflows, expert networks, and scalable service delivery infrastructure.

# Product Principles

The following principles govern all product decisions:

- Customer-first experiences
- Workflow-driven operations
- Configurable business processes
- Centralized operational control
- Secure data management
- Auditability by default
- Automation before manual intervention
- Scalability without major redesign
- Multi-role ecosystem support
- Partner-enabled growth

---

# Working Rules

- Product documents are the authoritative source of truth.
- UI designs are implementation artifacts, not business requirements.
- Confirmed business decisions override assumptions.
- Unresolved questions must be explicitly documented.
- Changes must update affected downstream documents.
- Duplicate or conflicting documentation should be removed.
- Product and architecture decisions must remain aligned.
- New services should be onboarded through configuration wherever possible.
- Platform extensibility should be preferred over hardcoded solutions.
